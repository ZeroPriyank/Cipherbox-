How it works ▶
1

Key derivation. Your password is run through PBKDF2 (SHA-256, 250,000 iterations) to produce a 256-bit AES key. The raw password itself is never used directly as a key.

2

Encryption. Your text is encrypted with AES-GCM, an authenticated cipher — meaning any tampering with the ciphertext is detected automatically.

3

Fresh randomness. A random 16-byte salt and 12-byte IV (nonce) are generated with crypto.getRandomValues on every single encryption, even for identical text and password.

4

Self-contained output. The salt, IV, and ciphertext are packed together and Base64-encoded into one copy-paste-safe block, so the Decrypt tab can pull everything it needs back out.

No network requests. No servers. No stored keys. Everything happens in this browser tab.
